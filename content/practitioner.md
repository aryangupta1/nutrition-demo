**Who I am and what I do:**

**Why (philosophy):**

I haven't given up hope that everyone can improve their health via nutrition - even in small ways." [A]

> "My work in health and nutrition enables me to place biology and chemistry at the heart of what I do, and ultimately to improve people's lives." [A]

**How I practice:**
> "I see every client as an individual who's with me for one main reason - they want the best for themselves. Which means we have to understand their individual needs and circumstances before developing their treatment plan and making health recommendations." [A]

> "If you have a medical condition, we usually start with a dietetic approach (medical nutrition therapy) which considers food-first solutions and then nutrition supplements to meet specific needs. For clients who have inter-related health problems, or when food alone is not effective, a more holistic (whole person) approach including naturopathic strategies can be considered. This could include functional health testing as well as dietary and herbal supplements." [A]

> "I encourage clients to plan for at least three sessions. The first session consists of assessment and information gathering. At the follow-up (second) consultation we setup the treatment plan. Progress is monitored at regular follow-up sessions, with the treatment plan adjusted where appropriate." [A]

## Services / areas of expertise
From homepage "Areas of Expertise" and /what-we-offer (fuller detail): [H, O]

1. **Digestive Health** — reflux and upper digestive symptoms, IBS, bloating and abdominal discomfort, food intolerances/sensitivities, constipation and diarrhoea, SIBO, gut microbiome-related concerns, general digestive health concerns. [O]
2. **Metabolic Health & Weight Management** — weight management, insulin resistance, prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol and triglycerides, fatty liver disease, metabolic syndrome. [O]
3. **Hormonal Health** — perimenopause and menopause, PCOS, thyroid disorders including hypothyroidism, hormone-related weight concerns. [O]
4. **Allergies, Intolerances & Immune Health** — food intolerances, histamine-related symptoms, allergic conditions, recurrent infections, immune health concerns. [O]
5. **Chronic Health Conditions** — cardiovascular disease, healthy ageing, osteoporosis and bone health, inflammatory conditions, complex health concerns. [O]
6. **Functional Health Testing** — pathology/functional testing related to digestive health, nutritional status, metabolism, hormone balance, stress response, genetic factors. Examples: gut microbiome testing, SIBO breath testing, vitamin/mineral testing, comprehensive thyroid testing, stress hormone testing, neurotransmitter testing, selected DNA testing. Note: "functional tests are not covered by Medicare and may involve additional costs." [O]
